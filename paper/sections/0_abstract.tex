\begin{abstract}
    The composed image retrieval (CIR) task involves retrieving target images based on a composed query of a reference image and modified text. Fashion retrieval applies CIR methods to the fashion domain, facilitating the search of clothing and accessory images. This task is challenging due to the scarcity of positive examples and the limited scope of negative sampling techniques. This study aims to improve the effectiveness of contrastive learning for fashion retrieval by introducing a multi-modal large language model to generate high-quality triplets and a two-stage fine-tuning framework. We leverage pretrained vision-language models, such as BLIP-2, to generate and concatenate sentence-level prompts with the relative caption. Our method scales the number of negatives using static representations. Future work may explore additional techniques to enhance negative sampling and further validate the applicability of the proposed methods across different domains.
\end{abstract}
